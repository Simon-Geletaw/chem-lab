# Lab coat — coat rack beside the exit door, E to wear (PPE layer 1)

**Date:** 2026-08-10 · **Area:** `src/coat.js`, `src/hand.js`, `src/gloves.js`,
`src/trashBin.js`, `src/ui/hud.js`, `src/zones.js`, `src/main.js`,
`src/ui/welcomeCard.js`, `public/draco/` (new)

## Problem

The repo already contained a downloaded coat hanger and white coat
(`/Models/coat_hanger.glb`, `/Models/white_coat.glb`, committed as "not yet
wired in") — but no code touched them, and the white coat is DRACO-compressed,
so the app's plain `GLTFLoader` could not even load it. The teacher wanted the
coat to be real PPE: the player sees the coat hanging by the exit on entry,
presses E at it (in the PPE area) to put it on — the coat vanishes from the
hanger and the HUD chip flips OFF → ON, exactly like the gloves.

## Design decisions (confirmed with the user)

- **Placement:** beside the exit door (front wall, x = +1.35) — in the clear
  gap between the simulation doors and the exit door, visible the moment you
  enter.
- **Enforcement:** like gloves — pickups of HAZARDOUS chemicals (hazard ≠
  none) are blocked without a coat (hint card); water stays reachable.
- **Removal:** the biohazard bin only, one item per press, order
  **gloves → mask → coat** (a fresh coat reappears on the rack).
- **Visibility:** white sleeves render on the first-person wrists, so wearing
  the coat is visible on your own body; nitrile gloves render over the sleeves.

## How it was done

1. **Vendored the Draco decoder** — `white_coat.glb` is DRACO-compressed and
   three's `DRACOLoader` needs its wasm decoder. Copied
   `draco_decoder.wasm` + `draco_wasm_wrapper.js` from
   `node_modules/three/examples/jsm/libs/draco/` into `public/draco/`, and
   `src/coat.js` wires `loader.setDRACOLoader(draco)` with
   `setDecoderPath('/draco/')`.

2. **`src/coat.js` (new)** — loads both GLBs and sizes them by *measuring*
   bounds at load time (same technique as the wall window in `room.js`): the
   rack is scaled to 1.8 m, the coat hangs from the rack's hook (top of the
   coat meets the hook point, centered in x/z) — no hand-tuned offsets. The
   coat's materials are repainted to soft lab white. Sleeves are open
   cylinders parented to the gloves' **roll groups** (newly exposed as
   `rightRoll`/`leftRoll` in `gloves.js` — the same anchor frame as the
   gloves' own cuffs), on hand render layer 1 so the hand lights keep them
   bright in the dim room.

3. **`src/zones.js`** — new `coat` zone `{ x: 1.35, z: 4.55, radius: 1.8 }`
   (the rack), which gates where E works on the coat (the same `inZone()`
   guard the glove/mask boxes use).

4. **`src/hand.js`** — three branches in the E-key FSM:
   - EMPTY + aiming the coat: put it on (zone-gated, "already on" guard);
   - EMPTY + aiming a bottle: hazardous chemicals are refused while the coat
     is off — the hint card names the rack;
   - EMPTY + aiming the biohazard bin: disposal order gloves → mask → coat
     (the coat launches from the chest).
   Plus the hover-hint line "[E] Put on lab coat".

5. **`src/ui/hud.js`** — third PPE chip `Coat: OFF/ON` with the same
   green/red signal colors.

6. **`src/trashBin.js`** — `dispose('coat', …)` with a bundled-white-fabric
   prop (three squashed blobs), same quadratic-arc flight as gloves/mask.

7. **`src/main.js` / `welcomeCard.js`** — the coat is created after the
   gloves (it needs their roll anchors), passed into the hand, stripped on
   the burn-restart flow, and the welcome card workflow now starts with
   step 0: "coat rack by the exit door".

## How it was verified

- **Build:** `npm run build` passes.
- **DRACO:** the coat GLB decodes via `/draco/` (vendored wasm, no network).
- **Numbers:** rack at (1.35, 0, 4.55), 1.8 m tall; clear of the sim doors
  (right edge −1.7) and the exit door (left edge 2.55); clear of the biohazard
  bin at (3.35, 0, 3.6).
- **Visual:** enter the lab — the rack + hanging coat sit beside the exit;
  walk over, aim at the coat ("[E] Put on lab coat"), press E → coat leaves
  the rack, sleeves appear on the wrists, chip goes green.
- **Rules:** a hazardous bottle pickup without the coat shows the hint card;
  with the coat on, pickup works (bare hands still burn per the existing
  T6 rule); the biohazard bin takes the coat off after gloves and mask.
- **Restart:** the burn-restart flow strips the coat too (chip + sleeves,
  fresh coat back on the rack).
